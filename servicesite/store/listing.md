## Full description

ServiceSite is the Android app for your StormBreaker website.

Open the dashboard, edit your page, and see the bookings customers send you. When you publish on the web, the app shows the same site. It is built for car washes, barbers, cleaners, tutors, and other service businesses.

The app does not sell plans. The Plan screen shows the plan already on your account. To buy, switch, or cancel a plan, use the ServiceSite website in a browser.

Support is on WhatsApp at +230 54834054.

To delete your account, message that number and ask. StormBreaker deletes the account and the personal data tied to it.

Privacy notice: https://stormbreaker.live/privacy

## Data safety

Match the privacy notice. The website behind the app collects:

- Name, email address, and phone number
- Business content the customer adds (text, photos, services, opening hours)
- Booking details their customers submit
- Support messages
- Usage and device data such as pages visited and IP address

Payment card details are handled by Paddle on the website. The Android app does not sell subscriptions and does not include a card form.

Do not mark the data as sold. Do not mark it as shared for advertising.

## Content rating
